    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: February 2026

    Tech Verse ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights over it.

    1. Who we are

    Tech Verse Ltd, Aston University, Birmingham, UK.
    Contact: privacy@techverse.local

    2. Data we collect

    • Account data: name, email address, password (hashed).
    • Order data: delivery address, order history, payment references (we do not store raw card numbers).
    • Usage data: pages visited, browser type, approximate location (country only) — only with your consent.
    • Communications: messages sent via our Contact Us form.

    3. How we use your data

    • To fulfil and manage your orders.
    • To provide and maintain your account.
    • To send transactional emails (order confirmations, verification codes).
    • To improve our service (analytics — only with consent).
    • To comply with legal obligations.

    4. Legal basis

    • Contract: processing needed to fulfil your order.
    • Legitimate interest: fraud prevention and site security.
    • Consent: analytics cookies (you can withdraw at any time).
    • Legal obligation: financial records retention.

    5. Cookies

    We use essential cookies (session management, security) and, with your consent, analytics cookies. You can manage your preferences at any time using the button below.

    6. Data retention

    • Account data: retained while your account is active.
    • Order records: 7 years (legal obligation).
    • Security logs: 90 days.
    • Deleted accounts: anonymised within 30 days of deletion request.

    7. Your rights (UK GDPR)

    • Access: request a copy of your data.
    • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
    • Erasure: request deletion of your data.
    • Portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format.
    • Restriction / Objection: restrict or object to processing.
    • Withdraw consent: at any time for analytics.

    8. Exercise your rights

    Sign in and visit your Account page to download your data or submit a deletion request. Alternatively email privacy@techverse.local.

    You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk.

    9. Security

    We use industry-standard security measures: HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords (bcrypt), session-based authentication, and regular security reviews. No system is 100% secure; we will notify you promptly of any breach that affects your data.

    We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via email or a notice on the site. Continued use of Tech Verse after changes constitutes acceptance.
